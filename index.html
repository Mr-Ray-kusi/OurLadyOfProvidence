About OLPSEC

A legacy of faith, learning, and leadership for young women in Ghana.

Our History

Our Lady Of Providence Senior High School (OLPSEC) was founded on November 19, 1989 by the late Bishop James Kwaku Owusu in partnership with the Sisters of Divine Providence. Situated in Kwasibuokrom, near Drobo/Japekrom in the Bono Region of Ghana, OLPSEC has grown into a Category A Day and Boarding institution — one of the top-performing girls' schools nationally.

Rooted in Catholic values and academic rigor, the school continues to nurture young women who lead with integrity, serve with compassion, and excel with purpose.
